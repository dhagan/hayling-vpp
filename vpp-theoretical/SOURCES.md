# Sources for the 505 theoretical VPP

**Accessed:** 2026-11-08

## Primary class-rule sources

The official-hosted [2024 Class Rules PDF](https://www.int505.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Class-Rules.pdf)
has PDF metadata creation date 2024-09-08 and title
`CLASS RULES - B - THE MEASUREMENT RULES`. Its text was checked for the rule
limits recorded in `parameters.json`, including Rules 1.1, 5.8.2, 6.2,
7.2–7.4, and 8.2–8.4. The hull-template values are separately attributed to
the hosted measurement form. However, the Association's [Class Rules page](https://int505.org/about-the-505/resources-for-sailors/class-rules/)
still links the older [2014 Measurement Rules PDF](https://int505.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/505MeasurementRules.pdf).
The 2024 PDF is on the official domain, but no later amendment or confirmation
that it is the latest consolidated edition was located. Check with the
Association or an authorized measurer before compliance decisions.

The measurement rules are particularly important because they state that the
hull shape must conform to the designer's drawings and offsets (Rule 5.2.1).
They define measurement stations and include hull templates/check dimensions.
Those controls are not a complete hydrodynamic offsets table or a full 3D hull
plan for every compliant 505.

## Rule extracts used in this input register

- Rule 1.1: 5.05 m LOA; design attributed to John Westell.
- Hull measurement form: 5050 mm overall hull length; station 3/6/9 template
  widths shown as 1350/1880/1790 mm, with stations 3810/2438/1066 mm forward
  of Station 11.
- Rule 5.8.2: 127.4 kg minimum dry sailing weight without sails and battens.
  This is not sailing displacement.
- Rule 6.2: centerboard protrusion below the hull may not exceed 1450 mm.
- Rules 7.2.1–7.2.5: mast position 3048–3202 mm forward of Station 11; mast
  spar cross-section max 102 mm; mast curvature max 30 mm; mast top max
  7011 mm above mast datum; upper limit mark max 6858 mm; spinnaker hoist
  5054–5955 mm; headsail halyard entry 4648–4750 mm.
- Rules 7.3.1–7.3.4: boom cross-section max 102 mm; curvature max 25 mm;
  outer-point distance max 2858 mm; overall length max 3308 mm.
- Rule 7.4.1: spinnaker pole length max 2516 mm.
- Rule 8.2.3: mainsail leech max 6960 mm, top width 127 mm, upper-leech width
  270 mm at a point 325 mm from the head, three-quarter width 1120 mm, and
  half-width 1950 mm.
- Rule 8.3.3: headsail luff/foot/leech maxima 4510/2287/4000 mm and top width
  40 mm.
- Rule 8.4.2: spinnaker leech/foot-median/foot/half-width maxima
  6000/7096/4500/4500 mm.

## Supporting performance/method sources

- [USA 505 — Getting Around the Racecourse](https://usa505.org/library/improve-your-505-sailing/getting-around-the-racecourse/):
  qualitative 505 sail/angle and course guidance, not a numeric polar.
- [North Sails — 505 Tuning Guide](https://www.northsails.com/en-us/blogs/north-sails-blog/505-tuning-guide):
  rig-tuning context, not a measured force coefficient set.
- [Dane Hull — VPP / Speed Sailing paper](https://www.foils.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/Speed-Sailing-Design-and-Velocity-Prediction-Program-Dane-Hull.pdf):
  methodological reference for a different boat class, not a source of 505
  coefficients.
- [International 505 class](https://int505.org/) and [USA 505](https://usa505.org/):
  class context; the class describes the boat as double-handed with one
  trapeze.

## Important gaps

- No full hull lines/offsets or approved hull plan for the target boat have
  been obtained. A target hull's designer/build records or measured scan are
  needed; the class templates alone are insufficient for a resistance model.
- No class-wide numerical righting-moment value was found. Righting moment is
  configuration-dependent and requires actual crew masses/positions plus hull
  hydrostatics.
- The rules give sail measurement limits, not the exact area/shape or
  aerodynamic coefficients of the target boat's sails.
- The 127.4 kg minimum is a rule-condition weight, not the VPP's all-up
  displacement. Measure the actual boat, rig, sails, equipment, and crew.
