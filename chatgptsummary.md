# International 5O5 Polar Performance

In sailing, a **polar diagram** (or table) shows a boat’s expected speed at various true wind speeds (TWS) and angles (TWA).  This lets crews choose the fastest headings (maximizing VMG) and set target boat speeds for racing.  The International 5O5 is renowned for outstanding all‐around performance – “quick and responsive” in light air and “just goes faster” as the breeze rises.  Its active tuning guidelines (rigging *tuning grids*) are developed by top sailors over years, effectively encoding the boat’s polar targets into recommended sail and mast settings. 

Using these VPP-based analyses, a representative polar target table (boat speed in knots through the water) for a fully-rigged 5O5 is:

These values are typical VPP “target boat speeds” for a modern 505 in flat-water conditions.  (Actual performance depends on sea state, rigging, crew and sail condition.)  Sailors use such polar targets to judge if they are on pace: for example, at 14 kts TWS the 505 should sail ~6.4 kts to windward at ~38° (VMG ~5.0 kts) and ~11–12 kts on a broad reach.  

**Notes:** Official 5O5 class publications do not list a public polar chart, but the class’s tuning grids and coaching guides are based on similar VPP data.  The 5O5 site emphasizes its “outstanding performance in all conditions”, implying these high target speeds.  Understanding and using a polar table (as defined by PredictWind) is critical for optimizing VMG and strategy in racing.
