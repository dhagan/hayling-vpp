# Comparative-class data workstream summary

**Updated:** 2026-11-09

## Scope and completed research

This workstream evaluates Flying Dutchman (FD), 470, and 49er material as
possible context for a 505 VPP. The source inventory, provenance caveats, and
access notes are in `DOWNLOADS.md`; transfer criteria are in
`TRANSFER_METHOD.md`.

- Downloaded 49er 2018 and 2020 `.pol` matrices and matching XLSX/ODS workbook
  variants. The matrices have 37 TWA rows and 15 TWS columns, but the index
  does not specify units or calculation method. Workbook instructions cite
  Regatta Game, so workbook data are game-derived; the `.pol` lineage remains
  unverified. These are not verified real-world polars.
- The IFDCO FD page provides dimensions, rig/sail areas, and hull/ready-to-sail
  weights. Its “up to 20 knots” statement lacks measurement conditions and is
  not a polar datum. A 2011 FD equipment review contains crew/equipment
  context, not boat-speed data.
- No raw numeric 470 polar was verified. The 2020 470 performance paper is a
  technical lead with access/redistribution limitations; Tactical Sailing
  exposes only graphical 470/49er profiles.
- Checked public polar indexes and Olympic/class tracking pages; no FD/470
  numeric polar or downloadable 470/49er track archive was exposed. Seapilot
  and SailGrib vendor pages were inaccessible (HTTP 403); no restrictions were
  bypassed.

## Use in the 505 model

Do not interpolate the 49er tables or scale another boat's speeds directly
into a 505 polar. Any comparison must remain a separately labeled, low-
confidence prior until units, conditions, configurations, and provenance are
established. No numerical 505 polar has been generated from this workstream.

## Project context

The 505 effort keeps observed 505 data, comparison-boat information, and
theoretical predictions separate. GPS-only tracks are not validated
true-wind/water-speed polars.
