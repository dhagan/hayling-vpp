# 470 / 49er comparison workstream

This workstream will evaluate whether public 470 and 49er performance data can
provide bounds or priors for a 505 polar. It is not a direct scaling exercise:
the boats differ in hull form, righting power, rig, sail plan, and planing
behavior.

## Intended approach

- Find published or measured polars with provenance, wind definitions,
  configuration, and conditions.
- Verify each source and record whether its speed is measured through water or
  over ground.
- Compare like-for-like wind and sail modes; do not merge values from different
  measurement conventions.
- Use comparative data only as a prior/range or reasonableness check for the
  505-specific model. Keep transferred values labeled and uncertainty explicit.
- Do not infer a 505 polar by simply interpolating the 470 and 49er tables.

## Status

The source/data inventory has not started. Theoretical 505 inputs and the
primary 505 model are being developed in the sibling `vpp-theoretical`
directory.
